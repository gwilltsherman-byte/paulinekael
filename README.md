# Kael

Kael is my film recommendation app. Rate films you have seen, and it uses those
ratings to suggest what to watch next.

[Open Kael](https://gwilltsherman-byte.github.io/paulinekael/) on your phone or
computer. You can use the app directly from that link.

## Start finding films

1. Open Kael and rate at least three films you have seen.
2. Browse the recommendations and open a film to see its details.
3. Keep rating films to help Kael learn your taste. Save films you want to watch.

You can import existing ratings in Settings. **Share my taste** creates a link
you can send to a friend to find films you both might like.

## Add Kael to your iPhone

Open Kael in Safari, choose **Share > Add to Home Screen**, then tap **Add**.
If Safari offers **Open as Web App**, leave it enabled. Open the Home Screen
icon before adding ratings so you keep using the same saved copy.

## Add Kael to your Mac

In Safari, choose **File > Add to Dock**, then open Kael from its new app icon.
Open that installed copy before rating films; it keeps its own saved data.
Closing and reopening Kael keeps your ratings. Once the app and catalogue have
loaded, saved film recommendations can open offline. Posters, trailers and
streaming links may still need the internet.

## Your ratings and backups

Kael saves ratings, your watchlist, diary and settings in your browser on this
device. They do not sync automatically between devices.

In **Settings > Backup**, save a backup before clearing browser
data or changing phones. Restore that file on the other device. Keep backups
private because they contain your ratings and saved films. Sharing a taste
link is optional.

## About this repository

The public [paulinekael repository](https://github.com/gwilltsherman-byte/paulinekael)
contains both the source code on `main` and this published app on `site`.
GitHub Pages serves the `site` branch. See the [installation and development
guide](https://github.com/gwilltsherman-byte/paulinekael/blob/main/RUNNING.md)
for saved ratings, backups and building a local copy.

Film data comes from MovieLens, IMDb and Wikidata. Posters, trailers and
streaming information come from TMDB, with streaming data supplied by
JustWatch. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified
by TMDB. The app is for personal, non-commercial use.
