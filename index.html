      Import your ratings

      From Letterboxd (export .zip, including your diary) or IMDb (ratings .csv). The more Kael knows about what you've watched, the better your picks.

      How to export

      Letterboxd: letterboxd.com → Settings → Import & Export → Export your data, then choose the .zip here.

      IMDb: imdb.com → Your Ratings → ⋯ → Export, then choose the .csv here.

      Backup

      Your ratings, watchlist, diary and settings are saved on this device only. Save a backup before you delete the app or switch devices, then restore it there.
