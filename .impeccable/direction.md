# Kael: title sequence

This is a substantial redesign of a functioning local-first film companion, built directly in its existing vanilla stack. The user explicitly delegated the choice of one direction and rejected style-choice rounds; no interview or decision board is required for this session.

## Direction contract

Product insight: the useful unit is a film plus the viewer's own opinion or the reason it is recommended. The existing full-screen mobile card hides that structure on desktop. Make the image and its meaning equally legible.

Film title sequences and subtitle typography supply the ink-blue / light typography relationship and the single gold emphasis. On working screens this becomes a navy masthead, cool pale reading ground, serif film titles, precise sans-serif controls, uncropped poster proportions, and quiet separators. No artificial critic's verdicts. Genuine model explanations form the signature: a title frame showing art beside the combination of rated films that supports the pick.

First viewport: masthead and five destinations, clear For you heading, one film with explanation and save/explore actions beside a Tonight entry point, then a visible beginning of the recommendation collection. Before three ratings the same space explains and offers the rating workflow. Desktop opens into a wide film-and-reason composition; mobile has a compact split recommendation, a reachable bottom nav, and focused poster rating.

Interaction: preserving the next quiz image behind the current one gives a fast, quiet dissolve. Every opinion updates locally; undo stays visible. Native controls, clear selected states, keyboard focus and protected dialogs carry repeated work.
